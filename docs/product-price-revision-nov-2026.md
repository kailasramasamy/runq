# Product price revision — 1 Nov 2026

Follows the farmer rate hike of 1 Nov 2026 (A1 stepped +₹4…+₹7 by FAT, A2 flat-rate
VMCCs +₹7, A2 FAT-based VMCCs stepped). Buffalo milk and buffalo products: **no change**.

## Basis

- **The landing price is what moves, by an exact amount.** Milk 500 ml: **+₹4.00**, i.e.
  ₹8 per litre of milk. Every product carries the same ₹8 per litre of milk it contains.
- Milk per pack: curd 400 g = 0.4 L · paneer 7 L/kg (200 g = 1.4 L, 500 g = 3.5 L) ·
  ghee 30 L/kg (100 g = 3 L, 200 g = 6 L, 500 g = 15 L) · butter 25 L/kg (200 g = 5 L).
- **GST is calculated after the increase:** the milk-content increase is added to the
  pre-GST (basic) price, and GST is charged on the new basic. Milk and paneer are 0%;
  curd, ghee and butter are 5%.
  `new basic = old basic + litres × ₹8` · `GST = new basic × rate` · `landing = basic + GST`
- **MRP rises by the same rupees as the landing price** (whole rupees, rounded down):
  the consumer pays the same ₹8/L more and the retailer keeps its ₹ margin per pack.
  The margin % is re-derived so `MRP × (1 − margin)` still lands on the new price.
  Packs: the plant adds the increase to whatever MRP is printed today.

## Does it pay for the farmer hike?

On October volumes at the master price (where nearly all invoices sit):

| | ₹ / month |
|---|---|
| Extra sales from this price list | **+1.79 L** |
| A1 farmer hike (7,040 L) | −0.44 L |
| A2 farmer hike (17,050 L) | −0.96 L |
| **Net** | **≈ +0.39 L** |

Milk packs carry most of it (A1 +₹53K, A2 +₹80K); ghee +₹26K, paneer +₹16K.

## Watch-outs

- **Retailer margin % drops** most on ghee (4amFresh QC A1 ghee 500 g 35% → 29%, master
  20% → 16%) because the rupee margin is held flat on a much larger price.
- **Ghee jumps 26–32%** (A1 500 g landing ₹399 → ₹525). That is the real milk content at
  30 L/kg, but it is the item most likely to lose volume. Milk packs alone already cover
  the farmer hike, so ghee could take a partial pass-through.
- `cost_price` on these items still reflects the old milk cost — update alongside.

## Rollout

1. Agree the new landing prices with 4amFresh, FreshFirst, BB Daily and FTH.
2. On 1 Nov update, per SKU: item master `default_selling_price` (landing), `basic_price`,
   `gst_value`, `mrp` (+ landing increase in whole rupees), `margin` (re-derived); each
   price-list entry's `mrp` and `margin_percent` the same way, set explicitly. Script:
   `apps/api/src/scripts/apply-dairy-price-revision-nov2026.ts`.
3. Raise `cost_price` by the milk content × the farmer increase.
